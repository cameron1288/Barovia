Doru's Rebellion

Doru raised a rebellion against Strahd, awakening him from his sleep. Six nights of horror followed across Barovia. Doru was turned into a vampire spawn and imprisoned in the crypt beneath Father Donavich's church. Father Donavich has not left the church since.

~6 Months Before Arrival

Stella Wachter's Soul Trapped

Lore
▼

While attempting to build a teleportation circle, Stella Wachter and Viktor Vallakovich triggered a catastrophic accident. Stella's soul was ripped from her body and trapped in the Ethereal Plane. She has roughly one week before she is lost forever — a ticking clock when the party arrives in Vallaki.

~24th–26th Octyavr — Day 1 | Sessions 1–2
Sessions 1–2

Arrival Through the Mists — Daguerfort Orphanage

MilestoneTravel
▼

The party's shared roots in the Daggerford orphanage were shaken when Grigor One-Eye revealed a mysterious cloaked stranger had been asking about them, brandishing unsettlingly specific knowledge of their past. He mentioned Zelly — a companion they grew up with — may have been the foster mother's true biological child.

Returning to the orphanage, they found it tied to the dark history of the Durst family — Gustav, Elizabeth, and siblings Thorn and Rose. The nanny Klara ("Lady Watcher") may have been the mother of the ill-fated Walter. Children's rooms still held stuffed toy wolves. Descending further, they discovered hidden chambers, defiled shrines, and Walter's crib inscribed with "He is the Ancient. He is the Land." They secured an amber amulet key as the house's full corruption was laid bare.

As the mansion collapsed around them, the party barely escaped, only to find themselves enveloped by the Mists of Barovia — all roads led only westward.

Sessions 1–2

⭐ Death House — Walter Slain

CombatMilestone
▼

Deep within the orphanage dungeon, the party confronted Walter — a grotesque abomination, the twisted child of nanny Klara and the product of the Durst family's dark rituals. Flint put the creature down permanently. As the source of the house's corruption was severed, the structure began a rapid collapse. The party's first true combat encounter in Barovia.

Klara's song was recovered from the keys of the harpsichord. Outside, the thick Barovian mists closed in immediately — delivering them straight into Strahd's domain. The trail ahead bore claw marks, paw prints, and a discarded tarnished copper compass (no magic). After forty minutes of travel they reached iron gates inscribed: "The Horseman Rides. The Seer Spoke True." Two more miles brought them to the village of Barovia — and the realisation they were now in the year 735.

~27th–28th Octyavr — Days 2–4 | Sessions 3–4
Sessions 3–4

Village of Barovia — First Encounter with Strahd

StrahdTravel
▼

At the Blood of the Vine Tavern they met Ismark Kolyanovich, who revealed Ireena has been targeted by Strahd. A mob led by Alenka was trying to surrender Ireena to appease the dark lord. They learned of Doru, turned into vampire spawn after a failed rebellion three months prior. The party met the salty-but-brave mercantile owner Biltrath Cantemere, his nephew Perry Wimple, and heard of the loss of Biltrath's sister Martha to the undead.

They attended the somber funeral of Burgomaster Kolyan Indirovich, led by a broken Father Donavich, who confessed he had given his holy symbol to Doru for the rebellion — leaving him devoid of faith. The party returned the holy symbol to Father Donavich, restoring his divine powers instantly. Beneath an ancient oak tree, they unearthed Van Richten's hidden cache: a tarnished moonstone bracelet with the note "Tatianna: May you find your path, and may you find light even in the darkest places. — Mother & Father." Ireena officially joined the party.

The party then escorted Ireena westward — only to be confronted on the road by Strahd's carriage driven by the vampire spawn Esher. Strahd himself emerged: pale, towering, ruby at his throat. He handed Flint a mysterious trinket to keep him busy and seized Vel's mirror. He insisted the Tarokka cards were instruments of deception before granting them their lives — for now. Vel fell into a trance and heard Strahd recite a dark counter-prophecy.

Then came the undead assault on Barovia village. The party fought alongside the locals including Biltrath. Rahadin arrived on a phantom steed — heralded by the fading screams of a thousand souls — and delivered a chilling ultimatum on Strahd's behalf. Mel's knife reacted unnervingly to Rahadin's proximity.

Sessions 5–10

Tser Pool — Madam Eva's Tarokka Reading

LoreTravel
▼

The party escorted Ireena westward to the Tser Pool Vistani encampment, where Madam Eva had been expecting them. At midnight at the River Ylivis Cemetery, she delivered the full Tarokka reading:

    At the Tser Pool encampment, the party played a Game of Stories with Arturi (an old acquaintance of Van Richten). Mel's victory earned them a brass spyglass, a scholarly manuscript on the werewolf's curse, a painted wooden hairclip (from Ireena), and a worn deck of Tarokka cards (from Stanomir). Stanomir bestowed the party with prophetic titles: Velastra — "The Star That Fell Twice", Spiggot — "Twice Born Exile", Ripley — "Masked Fang", Flint — "Echo of Gears", Mel — "The Quiet Watcher."

    At midnight at the River Ylivis Cemetery, Madam Eva delivered the full Tarokka reading. As the final card was revealed, the surrounding candles were extinguished and Madam Eva was seized by a possession-like tremor:

    • Card 1 — The Traitor: History / Knowledge of the Ancients — "The lost soul will lead you to him" (ancient foe of a noble house)
    • Card 2 — The Myrmidon: Holy Symbol of Great Hope — wolf's den in the hills overlooking a mountain lake; treasure belongs to Mother Night
    • Card 3 — The Bishop: Weapon of Vengeance (Sunblade) — amber prison where the devil fears to tread; House of the Fallen Dragon leads the way
    • Card 4 — The Mists: Ally — a Vistana wanderer searching for her mentor; seek her at Saint Markovia's Abbey
    • Card 5 — The Marionette: Enemy's Location — Castle Ravenloft's heights; the beating heart of the castle

    Madam Eva warned: "Be wise, don't believe everything you hear. Strahd received a reading this very morning." She also requested the party buy a Blinksy's toy for her great-grandniece Arabelle's 10th birthday in two days, delivering it to the Vistani camp southwest of Vallaki. Eliza (Vistani guard) joined the party.

~2nd Neyavr — Day 9 | Session 11
Session 11

The Bridge Knight — Argynvostholt Pointed

LoreTravel
▼

Travelling west from Tser Pool with Eliza now in the party, the group encountered a skeleton mounted on a horse holding an unlit lantern — travelling toward Barovia village. Wolves stalked them with unnatural intelligence. Flint was downed during an attack; a dark power offered him a hand and he accepted, restored to full health at a cost.

At the bridge over the Luna River they met a spectral knight — a member of the Order of the Silver Dragon who had forgotten his name in life, oath-bound to this location for having raised his voice against his commander. He asked if they opposed the lord of Barovia, then directed them: "Travel beyond the ruined town, then south upon the ash-grey path" to reach Argynvostholt and Ser Godfrey Gwilym for weapons against Strahd. His final warning: "Beware those who walk the skins of wolves to the west. Their hunger for human flesh cannot be sated."

Vel found a broken silver pendant shaped like a dragon. Flint confirmed no magic — but it was later identified as a relic of the Order of the Silver Dragon, dormant but bound to a greater command. A conspiracy of ravens began following the party.

✅ 13th Neyavr — Sessions 41 (Part 2) – 45
Session 41 — Part 2

Argynvostholt — Sir Godfrey Gwilym & the Order's Fall

LoreStrahdMilestone
▼

Escorted by a revenant knight, the party reached Argynvostholt and found Sir Godfrey Gwilym bound in barbed spectral wire within the Chapel of Mourning. He revealed the Order of the Silver Dragon's history: founded by Lord Argynvost (a silver dragon in disguise), the Order was driven back to the mansion by Strahd's invasion. In the final battle, Commander Vladimir Horngaard led a doomed last charge and was slain by Strahd himself; Godfrey fell to Strahd's second — Rahadin. Vladimir Horngaard's dying rage dragged the knights back as revenants. Strahd desecrated Argynvost's remains, burning his bones and taking his skull to Ravenloft as a trophy — hardening Vladimir Horngaard's hatred into an oath to let Strahd rot as king of a dying land rather than kill him, and forbidding the Order from ever striking at Strahd or his servants again. When the knights demanded to intervene during Doru's rebellion, Vladimir Horngaard banished the entire Order except Godfrey, whose own oath still binds him here.

Argynvost's dormant spirit has begun stirring again since Strahd's awakening, whispering of "a new generation of heroes." Sensing Flint's amber corruption, Godfrey grew suspicious of Amber Temple ties; Vel vowed the party would not let Flint fall further, and he relented — for now — but wants the shard cast into Lake Baratok. He will reveal the Sunsword's location only once the party completes Vladimir Horngaard's final mission: exploring Argynvostholt and speaking with its spirits, while avoiding Commander Vladimir Horngaard's unkillable revenant guard entirely. Funeral vestments from a mass grave south of Luna Lake are needed to move safely among the mansion's other hostile dead.

Session 42

The Mass Grave and the Hide-and-Seek Spirit

CombatLoreStrahd
▼

With the dinner at Castle Ravenloft one day away, the party gathered digging tools in Vallaki and headed south to the mass grave near Luna Lake. En route they found a giant wolf trampled to death, then tracked its killer to a dying colossal elk marked with the Huntress's three-pointed star, its wound unhealable by normal magic. Vel granted it a mercy killing before a mud-cloaked witch riding an undead dire wolf arrived and harvested its heart and liver, calling it "a lucky day" despite the elk already being dead.

At the mass grave, the party recovered funeral vestments — helmets, shields, a silver medallion matching Vel's broken pendant, and tattered robes — fighting off a pack of ghouls in the process. A captured ghoul, interrogated before being released, spoke in broken Common of a "Dark Master," a "circle of stones" that shakes when a "swamp woman" visits, and "a great wave from the mountains into a flow." Disguised in the recovered vestments, the party returned to Argynvostholt and explored further — an empty sarcophagus carved with a queen's effigy, a ruined parlor, a plundered kitchen — before chasing down and trapping a small, childlike, draconic spirit inside a sealed decanter, suspected to be some sort of spirit companion of Lord Argynvost. It has not yet been questioned.

Session 43

Zorya, the Rings of Andral, and Brother Marek

LoreStrahd
▼

The captive wisp was freed and identified by Sir Godfrey Gwilym as Zorya, Lord Argynvost's own pseudodragon companion — unable to speak, but able to guide the party to hidden clues throughout the mansion. She led them to two matching silver vow rings hidden in the wine cellar, then through the towers and a cemetery (where an unidentified well-dressed stranger briefly watched them from a window) to Argynvost's own bedchamber. There, a hostile spirit named Minadora — who died the same day Strahd's forces broke Argynvostholt, alongside Vladimir Horngaard and Godfrey — revealed that Brother Marek, a spirit lingering in the Ethereal Plane, may hold the key to freeing the mansion's trapped dead, if his prayer book can be retrieved and read aloud.

A stained-glass gallery depicted an angel resembling the Abbott bestowing a silver statuette on Saint Markovia, while an envious-looking man looked on. In a lead-lined vault, the party found Argynvost's final testament — written just before he died fighting Strahd in dragon form — along with a hidden cache including a letter from someone named Andral imploring Argynvost to make peace with Strahd, and a silver tablet bearing the Order's full oath. The party concluded Vladimir Horngaard's oath has done nothing but harm his own men, and means to make that case to him directly. First, they need Brother Marek's satchel, currently buried in a ballroom nest of seven giant spiders — Vel got a hand on it before the session ended with four spiders bearing down on her.

Session 44

Brother Marek's Prayer, the Two Lovers, and the Password at the Tower

CombatLoreStrahd
▼

A running battle against Argynvostholt's full spider nest let the party recover Brother Marek's satchel — his prayer book and two vials of holy water. Mid-fight, Flint began perceiving trapped spirit-essence bursting from each dying spider, unable to leave the room; his amber shard entity called the mansion "a closed system" where "the dead have nowhere to go," and his own instincts saw the spirits as harvestable, weaponizable fuel — a realization he's kept to himself.

Reading Marek's prayer aloud summoned his bitter, hopeless spirit from the Ethereal Plane, but Mel's genuine, furious honesty won him over. He named the vision's silver statuette the "Icon of Dawn's Grace" and told the party to "seek out the younger of the twin dragons above, reignite the silver flame it once wielded, reminded of the oath it once served." Following the clue, the party found Vladimir Horngaard's empty throne room, a war room with the Order's full oath carved into a round table, and a torn portrait of two men — matching Godfrey Gwilym and Vladimir Horngaard by build — clasping hands, suggesting the earlier vow rings are theirs. At a guarded tower, spectral archers stood down once Vel claimed allegiance to the Order and spoke its oath's closing line as a watchword — clearing the way to finally approach Vladimir directly.

Session 45

The Gargoyle's Prophecy and Flint's Grim Invention

CombatLoreStrahd
▼

The party finally confronted Commander Vladimir Horngaard face to face atop Argynvostholt's tower — his knight's armor dissolving to reveal a skeletal, red-eyed revenant. He flatly refused to release his oath, insisting it keeps Strahd trapped in eternal suffering, and turned hostile once pushed, forcing a chaotic escape via Feather Fall and a dash into the mist. Before fleeing, Zorya lit the tower's silver braziers regardless, waking its gargoyle just enough to recite a prophecy: the beacon can't be truly lit until Lord Argynvost's remains rest again in his mausoleum — remains the party now suspects Strahd keeps as a trophy inside Castle Ravenloft.

Back in Vallaki, Van Richten offered fragments of his own failed assault on the castle (a grand staircase, a "hall of bones," undead patrols, teleportation traps) and warned the party away from the Amber Temple without further insight. Meanwhile, Flint disappeared for hours to build and test a working prototype device capable of capturing the souls of dying creatures — successful, though he hasn't decided what to actually do with the power, or told the others what it does. Vel identified several of Argynvostholt's treasures as magical (a Moonlight Rapier, a Bracelet of Warding) before the party took a long rest, with Strahd's dinner invitation now just a day away.

Session 46

Reunions at the Gate, the Ancient Oak, and the Road to Castle Ravenloft

StrahdMilestone
▼

Father Donavich and Doru arrived at Vallaki's gate seeking Gertruda, nearly turned away over suspicion of Doru's lingering vampirism until Mel vouched for them. Doru gave the party their first eyewitness account of Castle Ravenloft's defenses from his own failed assault years ago: Strahd stepped straight out of a wall to ambush him and his companion Esher in the courtyard, as if he'd known they were coming. Separately, Aragel secretly passed Vel a note warning of "a spy in the castle" — suspicion falling on the long-vanished Esher.

Setting out at midday, the party's journey to the castle took an unexpected detour: the silent skeletal horseman that has watched the roads since their earliest days led them to a massive ancient oak marked "V & K," where they found a farewell letter from someone named Katerina to a recipient named Varushka, and recovered half a silver locket from a nest of hostile ravens. At the crossroads, Strahd's own carriage waited with a gift of a puzzle-locked chest (functioning as a bag of holding) containing tailored cloaks and gloves for each party member. They arrived at Castle Ravenloft itself as the session ended — crossing the drawbridge, passing beneath its gargoyles, and stepping into a silent courtyard with the actual dinner still to come.

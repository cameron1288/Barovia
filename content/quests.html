Fates & Active Quests

1
Reach the Amber Temple

Strahd told Flint: 'Amber Temple has noticed you. I know it.' Seriac's final message: 'Seek out the mountain, find the temple — let's hunt a vampire.' This is where Strahd made his original pact with the Dark Powers. It almost certainly contains the Sunsword (Tarokka Card 3: amber prison). Getting there likely requires going through Argynvostholt first.

2
Find the History/Knowledge of the Ancients (Tarokka Card 1)

The party has stumbled across the prophecy of the Card of History with Stella Wachter leading us to Leo Delinzia to the long-lost Tome of Strahd. By unearthing this ancient text, they have pierced the veil of Barovia's illusions and exposed the dark, tragic truths of the vampire lord's mortal life, his jealousy, and his blood pact with darkness. With the tyrant's deepest secrets and weaknesses now laid bare in their hands, this crucial thread of Madam Eva's fortune is officially resolved, forever changing how the companions face the master of Ravenloft.

3
Find the Sunsword (Tarokka Card 3)

The one weapon Strahd truly fears — confirmed by his own Tome: 'That cursed sword that Sergei brought! I must dispose of that awful tool!' Hidden in 'an amber prison, where the devil fears to tread.' Argynvostholt (House of the Fallen Dragon) is a required step before finding it. The Sunsword has a platinum hilt with a deep blue sapphire — a blade of pure sunlight.

4
Find the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind (Tarokka Card 2)

Retrieved from the werewolf den near Mother's Night Shrine in Session 41. Zuleeka Tornesku granted permission to take the amulet — a sun pendant with a large ruby — in exchange for a sworn oath. Mel took it and attuned; its full properties are still being identified. This resolves Tarokka Card 2, though the oath sworn to obtain it opened Quest 20 (Rescue Emil Tornesku).

5
Find the Tarokka Ally (Tarokka Card 4)

A female Vistana wanderer searching for her mentor — find her at Saint Markovia's Abbey in Krezk. She moves constantly. Most likely: Esmeralda Davenir, Van Richter's student, whose wagon was already at Kazan's Tower.

6
Travel to Krezk

Multiple reasons: (1) Tarokka ally at Saint Markovia's Abbey. (2) The blessed pool there has significance. (3) The Abbey itself may connect to other quests. (4) Zolt and Matilda (trappers from Krezk) are potential contacts.

7
Visit Argynvostholt

Sir Godfrey Gwilym found and spoken with in Session 41 — a revenant bound by oath, guarding the Chapel of Mourning. He will reveal the Sunsword's location only once the party completes "Vladimir Horngaard's Final Mission": exploring the mansion and speaking with its resident spirits, while avoiding the unkillable revenant Commander Vladimir Horngaard. Funeral vestments are needed from a mass grave south of Luna Lake to move safely among the mansion's other hostile dead. Godfrey also wants Flint to dispose of his amber shard in Lake Baratok.

8
Strahd's Dinner Invitation

Rahadin delivered Strahd's invitation in Session 27: dine at Castle Ravenloft in six days, safe passage both ways promised. Strahd also offered a separate reward at Yester Hill — meet him at the base of the whispering wall after the ritual. Almost certainly a test, a trap, or both. Refusing likely also carries consequences.

9
Strahd's Counter-Reading — Beware the Traitor

Vel overheard Strahd speaking in verse that appeared to mock or subvert the Tarokka reading: 'She thinks the cards speak truth, that rivers can be dammed / The Sword lies in the hand of the Dead / The Ally walks in the Shadow / Cast a light on the Traitor and call him friend / They will trust the wrong soul, let them chase a ghost.' Someone the party trusts may betray them. The Sunsword may be with a dead person. The ally may not be trustworthy — or may be hidden. Keep this in mind at all times.

10
Recover the 3 Winery Gemstones

Three enchanted gemstones that power 'Champagne de La Stomp' were stolen: (1) An unknown raven while Erwin failed to watch. (2) Baba Lasaga's scarecrows took the Red Dragon Crush gem. (3) The Forest Folk took the third. The Keepers of the Feather need them to maintain the winery and their resistance efforts.

11
Confront Baba Lasaga

The witch in the Ruins of Berez created the flying blight creatures, stole a winery gem, and has sworn to destroy the Martikov family. Her base may also be connected to the Swamp Fane — one of Strahd's three ancient power sources.

12
The Three Fanes of Barovia

Strahd's Tome confirms he claimed power from three Fanes: Swamp, Forest, and Mountain. This is why the land itself obeys him. Restoring these could significantly weaken his power. Likely locations: Swamp Fane near Ruins of Berez?, Forest Fane near Yester Hill?, Mountain Fane unknown?

13
Retrieve Franz's Children

Franz's children Josephia and Camel were found in a bookcase at Old Bonegrinder in Session 26. Were they actually freed and returned to him? Also need to address Franz's wasting disease.

14
Investigate the Sacred Tome of Strahd

Flint collected the Deep Crimson Red Tome in Session 29. Key contents: Strahd's personal history, obsession with Tatyana, pact with Death, fear of the Sunsword, and the three Fanes. The final page has a triangle symbol inscribed 'Exechanter'.

15
Restore the Bones of St. Andral

Recovered from the coffin maker's shop and returned to Father Lucian in Session 16. Church protection restored.

16
Kill Isek

Killed at Lake Zarovich in Session 23. Loot: Silvered Battle Axe, Amber Shard, 35gp, Studded Leather.

17
Save Stella Wachter's Soul

Ethereal Plane ritual performed in Session 28. Stella entered the binding circle. Viktor confirmed she was back in her body when the party returned.

18
Defeat Morganta and the Nightmare Hags

Morganta and daughters killed in Sessions 26-27. Heartstone retrieved. Franz's children found in the mill.

19
Winery Rescue

Spiggot destroyed Wand in Session 33. Every blight froze and crumbled. Martikovs returned safely. 2000xp.

20
Rescue Emil Tornesku & Restore Him as Pack Alpha

Sworn oath to Zuleeka Tornesku in Session 41 in exchange for the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind. The party must free Emil (Zuleeka's husband, the pack's deposed alpha) from Castle Ravenloft and help him overthrow Kirill, who usurped leadership 13 years ago using magic from Baba Lysaga. Deadline: the party's life force begins draining (max HP reduced by 1/7 cumulatively, -1 to all stats each day) starting the night after the New Moon on the 15th if the oath is unfulfilled. Zuleeka or another cleric of Mother Night could release the party from the oath early if needed.

21
Vladimir Horngaard's Final Mission — Appease the Spirits of Argynvostholt

Sir Godfrey Gwilym's price for revealing the Sunsword's location. Funeral vestments recovered from the mass grave in Session 42 let the party move disguised among Argynvostholt's hostile dead — two separate patrols of armored spirits have already passed them by without incident. Zorya (Argynvost's pseudodragon companion) and the spirit Minadora have pointed toward Brother Marek as key to freeing the trapped dead. The party has concluded Vladimir Horngaard's oath has done nothing but harm his own men and intends to make that case to him directly once they're ready — but he still cannot be fooled by any disguise and must not be seen, heard, or provoked until then. A nest of seven giant spiders in the ballroom remains a serious obstacle.

22
Dispose of Flint's Amber Shard

Sir Godfrey Gwilym demanded Flint cast his amber shard into Lake Baratok as a condition of his aid, distrusting anyone touched by the Amber Temple's corruption. Vel vowed on the party's behalf that Flint seeks only knowledge, not power, and would not be allowed to fall further — Godfrey accepted this for now but still expects the shard discarded eventually. Doing so could mean Flint's death, since his current existence depends on the shard's pact.

23
Identify the Captive Spirit of Argynvostholt

Resolved in Session 43: the trapped wisp is Zorya, Lord Argynvost's own pseudodragon companion. She cannot speak but understands Common and Draconic and has been guiding the party to hidden clues throughout the mansion.

24
The Swamp Witch and the Slain Huntress's Elk

A colossal elk bearing the Huntress's three-pointed-star symbol was found dying near Luna Lake in Session 42, its wound unhealable by normal magic. A mud-cloaked witch riding an undead dire wolf arrived and harvested its heart and liver, declaring it "a lucky day." A captured ghoul separately spoke of a "Dark Master," a "circle of stones" that shakes when a "swamp woman" visits, and "a great wave from the mountains into a flow" — possibly describing a ritual site tied to one of the Fanes, or to Baba Lysaga. Likely Baba Lysaga per the party's suspicion, though not yet confirmed in person.

25
Retrieve Brother Marek's Prayer Book

Resolved in Session 44 after a brutal fight against the ballroom's full spider nest. Brother Marek's prayer book and two vials of holy water were recovered and brought to the stained-glass gallery, where reading one of his prayers aloud summoned his spirit. Bitter and hopeless at first, he was won over by Mel's raw honesty and named the relic from the vision the "Icon of Dawn's Grace," pointing the party toward "the younger of the twin dragons" to reignite a silver flame and fulfill an old oath.

26
The Rings, the Letter, and Andral

Two matching silver rings inscribed "From this until the end of days" were found hidden in Argynvostholt's wine cellar. In Session 44 the party found a torn portrait of two men clasping hands, their faces worn away but their builds matching Sir Godfrey Gwilym and Commander Vladimir Horngaard — strongly suggesting the rings are theirs, not Argynvost's. Separately, the letter from someone named Andral imploring Argynvost to make peace with Strahd (found in the mansion's plundered vault, signed "Faithfully yours") now appears to be an unrelated historical thread — a political plea, not a personal one. Whether Andral's identity matters going forward is still unknown.

27
Reignite the Silver Flame

Zorya lit the tower's silver brazier beacons in Session 45, awakening the tower's gargoyle just enough for it to recite a verse: "When the dragon dreams its dream, within its rightful tomb, the light of Argynvost will beam and rid this land of gloom." The beacon apparently cannot be fully lit — and the curse over Argynvostholt's dead likely cannot be broken — until Lord Argynvost's actual remains are returned to his mausoleum on the grounds. The party suspects Strahd took the dragon's bones as a trophy after killing him; see "Recover Lord Argynvost's Remains."

28
Confront Vladimir Horngaard

Resolved (for now) in Session 45 — the party spoke with Vladimir Horngaard directly, revealed as a skeletal, red-eyed revenant beneath a knight's rusted armor. He refused outright to release his oath, insisting Strahd's eternal suffering depends on it, and turned hostile once pushed on the subject, forcing the party to flee the tower. He let slip that Argynvost "can't be heard" anymore — dismissing hope of the dragon's return — but the party isn't sure whether to trust that. Persuading him remains a dead end for now; the party is looking for another way around his oath entirely.

29
Recover Lord Argynvost's Remains

A tower gargoyle's prophecy in Session 45 revealed that Argynvostholt's beacon can only truly be lit once Lord Argynvost's remains rest again in "his rightful tomb" — the empty mausoleum found on the grounds. Massive claw-gouges on the tower and Van Richten's account of a "hall of bones" (likely a trophy room) inside Castle Ravenloft point to Strahd having taken the dragon's bones after killing him. With Strahd's dinner invitation the very next day, the party may get their first real look at where the remains are kept.

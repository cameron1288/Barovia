The Domain of Barovia

14 Southwestern Barovia
Argynvostholt

Ruined mansion of the Order of the Silver Dragon, founded by the silver dragon Lord Argynvost. This is the "House of the Fallen Dragon," a required step before finding the Sunsword. It lies south of the walled town on the ash-grey path.

  • Avoid Commander Vladimir Horngaard, the unkillable revenant who patrols the upper halls and the tower.
  • Wear the funeral vestments from the mass grave near Luna Lake. They let the party move past most of the other spirits, some hostile and some merely restless and bound by his oath.
  • Sir Godfrey Gwilym, bound in the Chapel of Mourning, is the ally here.
  • Argynvost's mausoleum in the walled cemetery behind the mansion is where his remains must go.
Areas explored (Sessions 41–45)
  • GroundA moss-covered statue of Argynvost stands before the entrance. Inside: a grand foyer with shattered busts, a dining hall lit by magical chandeliers, and the Chapel of Mourning (Sir Godfrey Gwilym).
  • GroundA ransacked bedroom with an empty sarcophagus carved with a queen's effigy, a ruined parlor with a mural of silver dragons and birds, a plundered kitchen, servants' quarters, and a wine cellar where two vow rings were hidden in an empty barrel.
  • UpperArgynvost's own bedchamber (a portrait of his human disguise, a dragon-carved bed); a lead-lined vault room stripped of most treasure but still hiding a cache behind a false panel; and a stained-glass gallery showing a vision of Saint Markovia and an angel resembling the Abbott.
  • BallroomA collapsed ballroom full of rubble and thick webs, home to a nest of nine giant spiders. Cleared in a brutal running battle in Session 44.
  • BeyondVladimir Horngaard's audience hall (an empty dragon-carved throne facing three tall windows); a war room with a round table bearing the Order's full oath carved into its rim; and a circular bedchamber with a torn portrait of two men, matching Godfrey and Vladimir, clasping hands.
  • TowerGuarded by spectral archers who stand down only for those who know the Order's watchword. Its peak holds a silver gargoyle brazier and is Vladimir's own post. The stonework bears massive, dragon-sized claw gouges, and a wrecked ballista nearby was aimed squarely at the same spot: evidence something enormous once fought or desecrated the tower.
  • CemeteryA walled cemetery behind the mansion holds Argynvost's mausoleum, guarded by silver dragon-wyrmling gargoyles, with four empty burial alcoves and an untranslated Draconic verse.
1 Central Barovia
Castle Ravenloft

Strahd's home, named after his mother Ravenovia. The party dined here in Session 47, and Strahd has promised them a tour.

  • Secret door: per Sasha, the dining hall organ's sixth pedal from the left opens a hidden chamber.
  • Not yet seen: the dungeons (where Emil is likely held), the "hall of bones" (possibly holding Lord Argynvost's remains), whatever is behind the great hall's east bronze doors, and the upper floors.
  • Dangers: an undead garrison (animated armor, phantom warriors, wights, zombies, spectres), reported teleportation traps and magical shortcuts, and a master who can step out of walls.
  • Strahd sleeps far below it among the dead, but will move to its heights when the party comes for him (Tarokka Card 5). His conquered wealth is buried beneath the belfry.
Approach & rumors
  • RumorBats constantly fly toward it.
  • VRVan Richten's secondhand account of his own failed assault: a grand staircase leads up from the main hall, and a "hall of bones," likely a trophy room, lies somewhere within. Strahd is rumored to pass through walls.
  • DoruConfirmed Strahd appeared to step directly out of a courtyard wall.
  • S46Twin broken stone turrets guard a fifty-foot, fog-filled chasm, crossed by a chain-drawn wooden drawbridge. Gargoyles leer from the high walls, and a rotted green portcullis opens onto a tunnel leading into a silent courtyard.
Areas explored (Session 47)
  • CourtCourtyard & entrance: A carriage roundabout in a small nook between two great towers. You can walk around the outside of the castle, but you can't see around the towers. Torches burn on each side of the keep's main doors, and above them is a round window whose iron frame still holds shards of broken glass. The carriage leaves toward the northern court gate, and the drawbridge is raised behind arriving guests.
  • FoyerEntry foyer: Vaulted, dark stone floor, with four dragon statues whose lifelike eyes flicker in the torchlight overhead. About twenty feet in is a second set of doors, kept by Esher and Sasha on arrival.
  • HallGreat hall: Cobwebbed columns hold up a vaulted, domed ceiling with four stone gargoyles squatting on its rim and cracked, faded frescoes. It is dimly lit by sputtering torches in iron sconces. East: closed bronze doors with no light beyond; Rahadin says they're "for later… if you are given the tour." North: a wide staircase climbing into total darkness. The servant Anton came down a torchlit staircase with drinks, and Strahd made his entrance down the grand staircase. South: a lit hallway with another set of bronze doors in a nook. The brides and Esher went down the southern stairs.
  • DiningDining hall: Behind red doors a short walk down the southern corridor. Three enormous crystal chandeliers, dull white marble walls with stone pillars, and a long table under white satin with fine china, silver, and crystal goblets of a fragrant amber liquid. There were more place settings than guests. At the centre of the far west wall, between floor-to-ceiling mirrors, stands a massive organ. Per Sasha, pressing the sixth pedal from the left opens a hidden chamber beyond it. Rahadin guards the doors during meals.
  • BelowServants' hall (downstairs): A long hall with heavy beams, a sagging ten-foot ceiling, mortared walls, and torchlight. At one end are heavy plank doors banded with steel. Invisible servants carry floating silver platters through them, and the doors close and lock behind. At the other end a raised iron portcullis opens onto the wine cellar. Three doors line the far wall beside a dark ascending staircase: the kitchen (middle), Cyrus's quarters (mops and brooms), and one still unexplored.
  • KitchenKitchen: Hot and smoky, with a whole pig roasting over a central fire pit and walls of hanging cooking tools that could double as torture implements. It is Cyrus Belleview's domain.
  • CellarWine cellar: Holds a dead village woman hung by her heels with her throat slit, her blood draining into a ceramic basin. This is the "wine" for the brides' and Strahd's goblets, and the staff treat it as routine.
31 Wolf Den
Wolf Den

A hidden lair in the hills above Lake Baratok where a werewolf pack resides. Zuleeka and Ilya are caged here, suppressed by silver-spiked collars. Emil Tornesku is the deposed alpha; Kirill is the current brutal pack leader, having usurped the position 13 years ago using magic from Baba Lysaga. The party swore an oath in Session 41 to rescue Emil and restore him as alpha, in exchange for the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind (already retrieved from Mother Night's Shrine). A reflecting pool inside forces visitors to confront visions of their true natures.

30 Near Luna Lake
The Order's Burial Ground

A mass grave revealed by Sir Godfrey Gwilym: south from Luna Lake, where the river meets the lake near an island, an ancient stone slab marks the way — 200 paces west lies the resting place of nearly a thousand fallen soldiers of the Order of the Silver Dragon. Funeral vestments taken respectfully from the dead (with the rite "May the day's light last guide you home") can disguise the party from Argynvostholt's maddened, hostile spirits — though not from Commander Vladimir Horngaard himself.

29 Luna Lake
The Peninsula Shrine

A small peninsula on Luna Lake bearing a mossy stone slab carved with a spider over a three-pointed star — the Huntress's mark. Site where a colossal elk sacred to the Huntress died in Session 42 from an unhealable, magically-cursed wound, its heart and liver harvested moments later by a witch riding an undead dire wolf. A raven kept vigil there throughout. Possibly connected to the ritual site a captured ghoul described nearby: a "circle of stones" that shakes when a "swamp woman" visits.

4 Central-Western Barovia
Vallaki

A walled town of roughly 1200 people. Now under Lady Fiona Wachter as Baronness. The old 'All will be well!' motto is no longer enforced. Founded over 500 years ago by Boris Valak. The Luna River runs nearby.

15 Northwestern Barovia
Krezk / Saint Markovia's Abbey

Walled town past the Raven River to the northwest, protected by 20-foot-tall walls. Features a blessed pool at the Shrine of the White Sun. Saint Markovia's Abbey is located here, where screams and laughter echo from within; it is managed by the Abbott, an ageless figure who serves as a healer and creator of abominations. Zelly has been encountered here. The village has struggled with werewolf attacks and a family curse affecting the Baron's line. It serves as a focal point for the party's current investigation into the Fanes and the creation of a bride for Strahd.

2 Eastern Barovia
Village of Barovia

The first village the party entered. Site of the undead assault and Kolyan's funeral. Father Donavich's church with Doru in the undercroft. The Blood of the Vine Tavern is the main gathering point. Kolyan found Ireena in the nearby Slavich Woods.

3 Central Barovia
Tser Pool Encampment

Vistani camp at Tser Pool, near Tser Falls. Madame Eva lives here. The Game of Stories and the full Tarokka reading happened here. Arturi, Slaromir, and Eliza are among the Vistani.

12 Western Barovia
Wizards of Wine Winery

Davian Martikov's winery and the Keepers of the Feather economic base. Three stolen gemstones powered its magical grape-growing. Druids defeated — Winter Splinter destroyed in Session 33. Has a hidden trapdoor in nearby woods leading to a cavern below.

16 Mountain region
The Amber Temple

Critical destination. Strahd confirmed it has 'noticed' Flint. This is where Strahd made his pact with the Dark Powers. The Sunsword (Tarokka Card 3) is very likely hidden here. Seriac/Norganas wants Flint here.

10 Northwestern Barovia
Lake Baratok

Remote mountain lake in the northwest. Kazan's Tower sits on a causeway into it. Van Richten was based here. The hills above it are most likely where the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind (Tarokka Card 2) is hidden in a wolf's den.

11 Lake Baratok causeway
Kazan's Tower

Wizard Kazan's tower on a causeway into Lake Baratok. Iron door inscribed with his name, radiating Abjuration magic. Gargoyles guard it. Esmeralda D'Avenir's wagon was outside. Van Richten's journal and the Tarokka card The Hooded One were found here. Kazan's magic staff may still be inside.

17 Southern Barovia (swamps)
Ruins of Berez

An old ghost-town in the southern swamps. Baba Lysaga is based here. She created the flying blights and stole the Red Dragon Crush gemstone.

13 Southwestern Barovia
Yester Hill

Ancient hill sacred to the druids. The Gultiest tree is here. Strahd appeared at the top on his Nightmare horse. Thirteen cloaked figures formed a circle at the base. A stone statue with fangs stood at the centre. The whispering wall is at the base of the hill. May be connected to the Forest Fane.

18 Road to Vallaki
Old Bonegrinder Mill

Morgantha's former home. Dream Pies were made here. Hags defeated. Franz's children found hidden upstairs. The party believes this and the 'Old Durst Mill' on the map are the same location referenced at different points in time.

19 Scattered across Barovia
The Three Fanes

Three ancient sacred sites pre-dating Strahd: Swamp Fane (Berez area), Forest Fane (Yester Hill area), Mountain Fane (Tsolenka Pass area). Strahd visited all three and claimed their power — 'thus I have become the Land.' Restoring them could break his connection to Barovia.

9 Vallaki outskirts (north)
Lake Zarovich

Large dark lake north of Vallaki. Ireena recognises it from a dream. Bluto the drunk fisherman uses it. Szoldar and Yevgeni's shack is on its shore. Isek was killed here in Session 23.

32 West of Vallaki
Vallaki Vistani Camp

A Vistani encampment just west of Vallaki, distinct from the Tser Pool Encampment further east. Home to Louvache and his daughter Arabelle, kidnapped from here during her 10th birthday celebration in Session 17 and later found safe in Rictavio's wagon. Louvache's brother Aragel owes the party a favor after they protected his role in the rescue.

5 Vallaki
Blue Water Inn

Run by Irwin and Danika Martikov — both Keepers of the Feather. Destroyed by Isek's rampage, presumably repaired now he's dead.

6 Vallaki
Church of St. Andral

Father Lucian's church. The holy bones were stolen and recovered — divine protection restored. Ireena sheltered here safely after the wanted posters went up.

7 Vallaki
Wachter Estate

Lady Fiona Wachter's home, now the seat of Vallaki's Baronness. The cellar sits on a Layline — used for the Ethereal Plane ritual. Stella is here, recovered. A terrible creature reportedly lives in the cellar.

8 Vallaki
Baron's Mansion

Viktor's room has his teleportation circle and the mirror where Stella was visible for months. Isek's attic was lined with Ireena dolls. Library holds historical faction records. Udo was chained in a closet here.

22 Village of Barovia
The Blood of the Vine Tavern

A popular tavern in the Village of Barovia, serving as a gathering place for adventurers and locals alike.

23 Tser Pool
Madame Eva's Wagon

The mobile home of the Vistani seer, Madame Eva, who provides guidance and prophecy to those who seek it. Last known location: Tser Pool Encampment.

24 Tser Pool
Tser Falls

A scenic waterfall near Tser Pool, often visited by travelers and adventurers seeking respite or information from the Vistani.

26 Lake Baratok
Esmeralda's Wagon

Esmeralda D'Avenir's rigged wagon, parked outside Kazan's Tower. Last known location: Lake Baratok.

21 Eastern Barovia
Svalich Woods

Dense forest surrounding the eastern approach to Barovia. Kolyan found Ireena as a child in the Slavich Woods — likely the same area. The Durst children recognised it as familiar.

20 Other Plane
Ethereal Plane (Barovia layer)

Entered via ritual in Fiona's cellar in Session 28. Stella is recovered. Erasmus Van Richten (ghost) lives here. A Gallowspeaker and Night Hag also haunt this layer. A binding circle surrounds the Wachter Estate in this plane.

27 Eastern Barovia
The Old Svalich Road

A treacherous road connecting various locations in Barovia, often patrolled by Strahd's minions and fraught with danger.

28 Throughout Barovia
The Svalich River

A winding river that flows through Barovia, providing a natural barrier and a source of life for the land, yet also a place of mystery and danger.

33 East Road to Castle Ravenloft
The Ancient Oak (V & K)

A massive, hundred-foot oak atop a rocky outcrop northwest of the main road, found in Session 46 when the party's familiar skeletal horseman led them off the path for the first time. Carved with the initials "V & K" at chest height, with a hidden hollow that held Katerina's farewell letter to Varushka. Two hostile ravens guard a nest near the canopy, where the party recovered half of a tarnished silver locket.

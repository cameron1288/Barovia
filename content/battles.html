Battles & Bloodshed

A ledger of every fight that's left a mark on the party — win, loss, or narrow escape.

Session 45 — Vladimir Horngaard's Wrath
Retreat / Narrow Escape Commander Vladimir Horngaard (revenant) Phantom warriors

Vel approached Vladimir Horngaard alone atop his tower, hoping to talk him into releasing his ancient oath. He listened just long enough for his knight's armor to dissolve, revealing a skeletal, red-eyed revenant beneath — and just long enough for Vel to spin a desperate lie about a plan (credited to "the Abbott") to torment Strahd with false, unrequited love instead of killing him. It worked, for a while. Pushed further on breaking his oath, Vladimir turned hostile, pinned the party against a wall of spectral wire, and marched on them with murder in his empty eye sockets.

⭐ Hero Play: Vel's improvised deception check landed a 28 — enough to keep an ancient, furious revenant talking well past the point he should have attacked, buying the party the seconds they needed to plan an exit.
⭐ Hero Play: Mel's Feather Fall turned a desperate leap off the tower's edge into a clean escape, drifting the whole party past a phantom-warrior ambush that was waiting for them below.

Fun detail: Before running, Vel had already whispered to Zorya to light the beacon anyway — meaning the party's "retreat" still triggered the gargoyle's prophecy about Argynvost's rightful tomb. A loss that turned out to be a win.

Session 44 — The Ballroom Spider Nest
Victory 9× Giant Spiders

What started as Vel quietly reaching for Brother Marek's satchel with Mage Hand turned into a running battle against Argynvostholt's entire spider nest — nine giant spiders in total, pouring through doorways and dropping from the ceiling as the party held the line across multiple rounds of poison, webbing, and attrition.

⭐ Hero Play: The whole party's sustained defensive effort — no single dramatic crit, just round after round of holding a chokepoint against a nest three times the size anyone expected.

Fun (and unsettling) detail: Mid-fight, Flint began perceiving something no one else could — each dying spider released a small, aimless spirit that couldn't leave the room. His amber shard entity called the mansion "a closed system" where "the dead have nowhere to go," and Flint quietly filed away the idea that those trapped souls could be harvested. He didn't mention it to the others.

Session 42 — Ghouls at the Mass Grave
Victory Ghoul pack

While the party dug up funeral vestments from the Order's mass grave south of Luna Lake, a pack of ghouls surged out of the fog and the graves themselves. Mel put his freshly-claimed Holy Symbol of Ravenkind to work turning several of them outright, while the rest of the party cut down what remained.

⭐ Hero Play: Mel's first real use of the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind — turning multiple ghouls in a single showing, less than a session after taking the artifact from Zuleeka.

Fun detail: The party captured the last ghoul and interrogated it before letting it go with a scrap of meat as payment. Its broken-Common ramblings about a "Dark Master," a "circle of stones," and a "swamp woman" remain an unsolved thread.

Earlier Battles — Sessions 1–40 (pulled from session summaries; full blow-by-blow not available)

Sessions 1–2 — Death House: Walter Slain
Victory Walter (abomination)

The party's very first fight in Barovia. Deep in the Durst orphanage's dungeon, the party confronted Walter, the twisted child of nanny Klara and the Durst family's dark rituals.

⭐ Hero Play: Flint put Walter down for good — the act that severed the source of the house's corruption and triggered its collapse, and the moment that pulled the party into Barovia in the first place.

Sessions 3–4 & 6 — Undead Assault on Barovia Village
Mixed — Village Held, Burgomaster Lost Undead horde

Barovia village came under a sustained undead assault following Doru's failed rebellion. The party fought alongside locals including the shopkeeper Biltrath Cantemere, who "proved his mettle" in the defense. Despite holding the line, Burgomaster Kolyan Indirovich was killed — clearly Strahd's doing — and Rahadin arrived afterward on a phantom steed to deliver a chilling ultimatum.

Session 12 — Werewolf Ambush on the Road to Vallaki
Mixed — Won, but Costly 5 Wolves (2 Werewolves)

An ambush by unnaturally intelligent wolves left both Vel and Spiggot bitten with wounds that refused to heal (cured much later by Mel). Flint was downed mid-fight — the first time a dark power offered him a hand, restoring him at a cost he'd keep paying for many sessions to come.

⭐ Hero Play: Blue, a raven-shifter ally, joined the fight and helped turn the tide before vanishing at the sight of bats.

Sessions 15–16 — The Coffin Maker's Vampire Nest
Mixed — Bones Recovered, Vampires Escaped Ludmilla Anastasia Volenta

Investigating the stolen Bones of Saint Andral led the party into Henrik Vandervoort's coffin shop — and straight into a vampire spawn nest. Vel took a poisoned dart to the chest before the three vampires (who mockingly called the party "skinny new plaything") were driven off. Volenta escaped as a bat through a crack in the wall; Ludmilla and Anastasia fled as well.

Result: The bones were recovered and returned to Father Lucian, restoring the church's divine protection — a win despite letting all three vampires live to reappear later.

Session 21 — Kazan's Tower: Gargoyles
Victory Gargoyles

Forcing entry into Kazan's abjuration-warded tower triggered an attack from the gargoyles guarding it. The party pushed through to find Van Richten's burned journal and the Tarokka card "The Hooded One" inside.

Session 23 — Isek Poisoned at Lake Zarovich
Victory (Assassination) Isek Strazni

Less a battle than a perfectly executed hit. Vel swapped Isek's wine for a poisoned decoy during his morning rounds; the party tracked him to Lake Zarovich, waited for the poison to take hold, and finished him off once he was weakened.

⭐ Hero Play: Vel's wine swap in the Baron's kitchen — the entire plan hinged on that one successful sleight of hand.

Sessions 26–27 — Morgantha & the Nightmare Hags
Victory Morgantha Nightmare Hags (her daughters)

Morgantha's daughters transformed into full Nightmare Hags at Old Bonegrinder Mill. Viktor countered one of Morgantha's spells at a critical moment, and the fight ended with Morgantha's Heartstone in the party's hands.

⭐ Hero Play: Flint finished Morgantha off with a critical hit from an upcast Guiding Bolt.

Session 29 — Leo Delinzia, the Lich
Victory Leo Delinzia (Lich)

A battle through the Ley Line against the lich Leo Delinzia — the former head of Strahd's castle guard, twisted by centuries of hiding and grief. Destroying his enchanted ruby was the key to finally ending him.

⭐ Hero Play: Flint landed the final blow (as he had with Morgantha), then claimed the Deep Crimson Red Tome of Strahd from the lich's remains.

Sessions 31–33 — The Winery: Blights, Druids & the Winter Splinter
Victory — 2,000 XP Needle/Vine/Twig Blights 4 Druids Elder Druid (Winter Splinter wand)

A multi-session siege through the Wizard of Wines winery, floor by floor, against waves of blight creatures and druids protecting a massive, blood-sapped tree that had taken root in the courtyard.

⭐ Hero Play: Spiggot shattered the Elder Druid's wand — the Winter Splinter — with his axe. Every blight in the area instantly froze and crumbled, and the corrupted tree dissolved into grey sludge as cleansing rain fell.

Session 34 — Strahd Kills Flint at Yester Hill
Tragedy → Amber Pact Strahd von Zarovich

Not really a fight — a one-sided punishment. Spiggot refused to show Strahd the deference he demanded ("We do not serve the lord"), and Strahd killed Flint on the spot to make the point. Isek's amber shard began orbiting Flint's body moments later, offering him a choice: die normally, or accept the pact.

Result: Flint chose the shard. He came back without a heartbeat or breath, and hasn't been quite the same since.

Session 34 — Yester Hill Ritual Combat: Ludmilla & the Ritualists
Victory — Ritual Disrupted Ludmilla (vampire bride) Druids & berserkers

Immediately after Flint's death and resurrection, the party fought through thirteen cloaked ritualists at the base of Yester Hill to stop a ceremony empowering the corrupted Gultiest tree, while Strahd watched from his Nightmare, treating the whole fight as entertainment. Ludmilla fled once the ritual was broken.

104 Hostile
Strahd von Zarovich
Castle Ravenloft
🖼 Portrait

The vampire lord and absolute master of Barovia. He plays the gracious, curious host and toys with the party rather than fighting them. Without the Sunsword he is effectively unkillable.

  • Weaknesses: fears the Sunsword (Sergei's wedding gift) "as much as the sun." It and holy sunlight are essentially his only real weaknesses.
  • Holds Emil prisoner to keep Kirill, "a useful brute," in charge of the wolves: "Hunger teaches obedience."
  • Doesn't know where Van Richten is, after Van Richten's recent attempt on his life.
  • Knows the party is hiding Ireena ("I trust she is safe"), and believes she is Tatyana reborn.
  • Calls Rahadin "my brother in all but blood." Wants a fifth bride and still longs for "the one" who eludes him.
  • Don't confront him until the Sunsword, the Holy Symbol (already retrieved), and a real plan are in hand. He sleeps far below the castle and will retreat to its heights when finally confronted (Tarokka Card 5).
History with the party (Sessions 3–47)
  • S3–4Appeared on the road out of Barovia village, toying with the party rather than attacking.
  • S9Appeared again near Tser Pool, still just toying with them.
  • S27Sent a formal dinner invitation via Rahadin for the 14th of Neyavr.
  • S34Personally killed Flint at Yester Hill as punishment for Spiggot's defiance, then offered the Amber Pact, which Flint accepted.
  • OftenHas repeatedly appeared to watch or comment on the party's progress (the Yester Hill rituals, the Whispering Wall, the Krezk road) without ever committing to a real fight.
  • S47Hosted the dinner at Castle Ravenloft. He came down the grand staircase to light applause in a fitted scarlet vest, white shirt, and long black velvet cloak while every vampire in the hall bowed. He was evasive about anything that mattered and drank blood from his own goblet all evening. Full detail in Lore: Strahd's Dinner Table Revelations.
  • S47Said he invited them because "few have come through the mists and survived the week." Called them his "prisoners," then corrected himself to "newcomers."
  • S47Said the mists obey him "most of the time" but are "not mine to open — not yet," and admitted "there are some things I cannot do."
  • S47Said he conquered "Old Zavoria" with his late father King Barov's army, and named much of the valley after his mother, who died when he was young. A dark bargain was clearly a sore subject.
  • S47Admitted visiting the Amber Temple "when I was young and grieving" and paying "the price gladly." He won't name the price. He says it lies "deep to the south" and "reveals itself to the worthy."
  • S47Of his brides: "each is a jewel; some have simply lost their shine." Says Volenta and Ludmilla act on their own, believing the party's deaths would please him, and he hasn't told them otherwise.
  • S47Said he slept "400 years or more" and was woken by an invasion of his castle. All but admitted turning Doru ("Could be").
  • S47Fished for news of Van Richten, saw through Mel's lie about never meeting him, and let it slide.
  • S47Probed each PC in turn (see Companions) and ran his "Game of Three Questions" to profile how they would rule. Invited Ripley to ride with him as a general. Promised a tour of the castle and dungeons after dinner.
Background

Once a great warrior-king who conquered this valley for his father Barov; years of war hollowed him out long before he became undead. He murdered his younger brother Sergei and made a pact with Death on Sergei's wedding day, after Sergei's betrothed, Tatyana, chose Sergei over him. Tatyana fled and fell to her death. Strahd has hunted her reincarnations ever since, and believes Ireena is his latest chance to have her.

017 Hostile
Rahadin
Castle Ravenloft (first met in Barovia Village)

A striking, dusk-skinned elf and Chamberlain of Castle Ravenloft: Strahd's voice, enforcer, and keeper of decorum. Strahd calls him "my brother in all but blood, the most loyal creature I've ever had."

  • Has served at Strahd's side for centuries and never tried to rule while Strahd slept.
  • Killed Sir Godfrey Gwilym in the Order's final battle. Deadly with a blade, and his hand drifts to it at any slight.
  • Mel's hidden blade reacts to him, pointing at him like a compass needle (pointing, not pulling) and humming harder whenever he's discussed. He has never acknowledged it.
  • Guards the dining room doors during meals.
History with the party (Sessions 3–47)
  • S3–4Arrived on a phantom steed during the undead assault on Barovia village. Marched in as his master's direct voice to deliver a grim message and a dire warning to the cowering townspeople.
  • S47Greeted the party at the castle doors in a rich blue tunic with gold spiral embroidery, wearing a permanent frown with his hands clasped behind his back.
  • S47Enforced "a certain decorum" like a drill sergeant: Vel's gown was "adequate," Flint got a nod, and Mel's armor and shield got an openly disdainful lecture. Asked why his own clothes were acceptable: "I am not obliged to answer you — but for the record, I am not a guest." Sent the carriage off with a click of his tongue.
  • S47When Vel pushed about which bride was the favourite, his hand drifted toward his blade: "I ask that you keep your manners at ease." Refused to say what's behind the great hall's east bronze doors ("I do not want to spoil any surprises that my master might have").
  • S47Announced dinner, pulled Vel's chair when Strahd prompted him, and stood guard at the dining room doors. Stepped forward when Mel berated Strahd, and Strahd waved him down. Silently agreed when Strahd called him a brother.

The party battled him through the Ley Line in Session 29 and came away with the Sacred Tome of Strahd (Tarokka Card 1); the Tarokka reading had named him "the ancient foe of the old and noble house." Also recorded as "Leo Delinzia."

Tatyana
Castle Ravenloft (long ago)

Sergei's betrothed, who loved Sergei and called Strahd only "Old One" and "brother." After Strahd murdered Sergei on their wedding day, she fled in despair and threw herself from the castle walls into the mists; no trace was ever found. Strahd has hunted her reincarnations ever since ("She cannot run forever — and I have nothing but time"), and his treasure is sealed "behind a reminder of the treasure I once lost." Ireena is very likely her latest reincarnation.

134 Deceased
King Barov von Zarovich
Strahd's father (long ago)

Strahd's father, "the great king, general," in whose name Strahd conquered this valley. Per Strahd, after Barov died and could not bring order, Strahd led his father's army to retake "Old Zavoria." Brother Marek's journal says the Sunsword was forged for King Barov's line and passed down to a younger son — Sergei.

Ravenovia

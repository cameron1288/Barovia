The Adventuring Party

The souls bound by destiny to navigate the mists of Barovia.

Mel (Paul / Broken Blade)

Cleric — Healer

The party's primary healer, and the keeper of the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind (Tarokka Card 2).

  • His hidden knife reacts to Rahadin, pointing at him like a compass needle and humming harder whenever he's mentioned. His sword may come from the same elvish tradition as Rahadin's longsword, both once used in service to the Zarovich family.
  • Swore Mother Night's oath to Zuleeka to free Emil. The deadline is the day after the new moon.
  • Has been researching the Sunsword, and knows Sergei von Zarovich once wielded it.
  • Strahd's vampires recognize the Holy Symbol: Anastrasya reacted to it with fury.
🖼 Items & trinkets
How others see him
  • FigureThe "Dark Figure" at the Inn: "Melda," the Quiet Watcher. Carries the weight of much blood. Silent Reckoner.
  • Strahd"You wear the sun upon your chest, yet you have the eyes of a man who has done his own share of darkness." Mel answered that he does her bidding, and his actions say more than his words.
  • StrahdOn seeing him skip the gifted formal wear: "I know all about you, Mel. I'm not surprised you did not wear my present." Strahd singled out both the sun symbol and a darkness in Mel's past.
History
  • VallakiLed the investigation into the stolen St. Andral bones. Discovered Isek's room full of Ireena dolls, and performed a ceremony after Isek was killed.
  • CuresCured both Spiggot and Vel of their werewolf bites with Remove Curse and Greater Restoration.
  • WineryIn the winery tunnels, stabbed himself with a runic wolf tooth to produce a blast of cold mist: a practical way to clear the yellow moss, nothing more.
  • S41Swore Zuleeka Tornesku's oath and claimed the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind, a sun pendant set with a large ruby that Mother Night herself wore for decades. His eyes briefly burned yellow as he took it.
  • S47At Strahd's dinner: refused the formal wear and kept his shield. Caught Anastrasya's hand as she reached for the Holy Symbol and read her furious recognition of it. Stopped Volenta from killing the servant Anton, needled Strahd relentlessly, and told a flat lie about never meeting Van Richten, which Strahd saw through and let pass.

Velastra (Vel) (Jocelyn / Stone Crest)

Bard

The party's quick-witted bard and diplomat, working with Disguise Self and Detect Thoughts.

  • Holds an unspent cryptic boon: a brief insight from the DM, earned in Session 47.
  • Went inside the Whispering Wall and learned a song there. What happened inside is "for me and the entity in the wall."
  • Carries Mother's amber shard (Norganas), Flint's original shard, which she picked up after Flint died at Yester Hill (Session 34) and came back bound to Isek's.
  • Carries a Moonlight Rapier, a Bracelet of Warding, and Arabelle's amulet of protection. Found a broken silver dragon pendant and a matching silver medallion.
  • Strahd seems taken with her, and his brides now openly hate her.
🖼 Items & trinkets
How others see her
  • FigureThe "Dark Figure" at the Inn: the Star That Fell Twice.
  • Strahd"There's something of the moon in you, my dear. Something older. Who taught you to sing like that?" She said she'd learned many songs on her travels, and inside the Wall.
  • StrahdAfter she played the song from the Wall on the dining hall organ, he rose and slow-clapped: "She is quite magnificent, isn't she?" Her boast that she looked more ravishing than all his brides combined drew his approval ("Extravagant, might I add… take some notes") and the brides' open hatred.
  • RictavioAsked about Blue, he said: "His heart belongs to someone else. His tell is when his eyes flip upwards."
History
  • CampWon a brass spyglass in Arturi's Game of Stories at the Vistani camp and gave it to Flint, who still carries it.
  • S9Had a crucial vision during a trance: Strahd's counter-reading (see Lore).
  • RoadBitten by a werewolf on the road to Vallaki; later cured by Mel's Greater Restoration.
  • VallakiUsed Detect Thoughts to break the St. Andral investigation open. Swapped Isek's wine with the poisoned decoy. Disguised herself as Isek to retrieve the Ireena doll from Blinsky. Was abducted by Isek. Received Arabelle's amulet of protection from Rictavio.
  • S42Found a silver medallion at the Order's mass grave matching her broken silver dragon pendant. Mercy-killed a dying, Huntress-marked elk before a mysterious witch could take it alive.
  • S45Talked her way face to face with Commander Vladimir Horngaard and nearly persuaded him with an improvised lie about tricking Strahd with false love, before he turned hostile. Identified her rapier (Moonlight Rapier) and a silver bracelet (Bracelet of Warding) by ritual.
  • S46Secretly received Aragel's note warning of a spy in Strahd's court. Recovered half of a silver locket from a raven's nest.
  • S47Led the party's diplomacy at Strahd's table, pressing him on the Amber Temple, his lost love, and the party's missing Mother. Passed her sanity check by more than five and earned the cryptic boon.

Ripley (Jon / Half-one / Scarf)

Half Orc

The party's wild card: scarred, blunt, and hard to read. He is addicted to Dream Pies.

  • Strahd seems to see a kindred warrior in him, and invited him by name to ride as one of his generals. Ripley declined, citing "prior engagements."
  • Has an instinct for wrongness: he sensed something off about Morgantha before anyone knew what she was, and saw a girl's face in Viktor's mirror that Spiggot couldn't see.
  • His sanity-save success gives allies who witness it +1d4 on their next d20.
🖼 Items & trinkets
How others see him
  • FigureThe "Dark Figure" at the Inn: the Masked Fang, who wears shame as armor.
  • Strahd"Tell me about the scars. I like a good fighting story." Ripley: "Not tonight. Tonight's not about fighting." Strahd: "That's too bad. The world spat on you, and yet you stand. I respect that more than you know."
  • StrahdAfter admitting the "hypothetical" rebellions and assassins were his own history, Strahd asked for volunteers to be his generals and invited Ripley to ride with him.
History
  • VallakiFound Udo the shoemaker chained in a closet in the Baron's mansion; the noise when he attacked the chains alerted a woman nearby. Saw a girl's face in Viktor's mirror.
  • MillWent upstairs alone with Morgantha's daughter Bella at Old Bonegrinder Mill.
  • VallakiAfter Vel's abduction, retraced her steps and found an abandoned house. Received a stuffed doll named Maud from Vel.
  • S47Ditched his armor-hiding suit for dinner because it was too tight. The masked servant silently refused to pour him blood. Passed his sanity check when Strahd raised Van Richten's name.

Spiggot (Jason / Feather)

Fighter / Warrior / Reborn

The Twice Born Exile: a warrior who carries a memory of a past life on the battlefield, and the party's most open defier of Strahd.

  • A past life he can barely recall: all that comes back is darkness, a boulder, and a tunnel. Strahd says he fights "like a man who has died more than once."
  • His defiance of Strahd at Yester Hill is what got his foster brother Flint killed.
  • Defining moment: smashing druid leader Loghoth's enchanted wand with his axe, which froze and crumbled every blight in the area.
🖼 Items & trinkets
How others see him
  • FigureThe "Dark Figure" at the Inn: Mountain and Battlefield, two men at war with each other.
  • StrahdAfter Spiggot called the valley "desolate" and said he's never happy: "You're a sad man. Death has taken the strength and will from you." Spiggot: "But it gave my brothers to me."
  • Strahd"Spiggot, the barbarian. You fight like a man who has died more than once." Spiggot described the darkness, the boulder, and the tunnel. Strahd: "A man who truly believes in nothing." Spiggot: "Nothing comes from nothing."
  • Strahd"You're a struggle within a struggle." Spiggot turned it back on him: "Like your castle that you can't fill."
History
  • BridgeTold the spectral bridge knight "We do not serve the lord," committing the party against Strahd without hesitation.
  • S27Bitten by a werewolf; fully cured by Mel.
  • VallakiSaw nothing when Ripley reported a girl's face in Viktor's mirror.
  • S33Destroyed Loghoth's wand with his axe (2000 XP).
  • S34Defied Strahd to his face at Yester Hill; Strahd killed Flint in response.
  • S47Bowed to Strahd facing the wrong way and was straightened out personally ("You seem to be missing a glove"). Knocked over Anton's tray and spilled blood across the great hall, setting off Volenta's frenzy. Answered the Game of Three Questions with an axe every time, and told Strahd flatly: "I don't listen to whispers."

Flint (Cameron / Echo of Gears / Amber Amulet)

Artificer / Inventor

An inventor pulled between his craft and the growing influence of the entity in his amber shard. Killed and brought back through the Amber Pact.

  • Secret meeting pending: Sasha told him to linger after dinner and press the organ's sixth pedal from the left.
  • Amber shard entity: bound to Isek's shard (Seriac, "Master of the Hunter") since Yester Hill. Its latest offer, Desperate Hunger, wants him to become a predator. His blood has turned black, and his left arm is turning red with barbed spines.
  • His original amber shard from Mother (Norganas, "Finger of Oblivion") is now carried by Vel.
  • Soul-capture device worn across his belt and back. He insists it's only for helping the party in combat, never for use on people.
  • Carries the brass spyglass (from Vel) and the Sacred Tome of Strahd (Tarokka Card 1).
  • Strahd's eyes go to him whenever the Amber Temple comes up: "Amber Temple has noticed you. I know it."

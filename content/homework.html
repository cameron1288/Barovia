Updated after Session 51. Next up: the Ravenloft heist, tonight while Strahd is away (until about 5 a.m.). These are the people and situations most likely to come up, with what we already know, so nobody has to stop the scene to ask. Tap a card to read it. Most likely first.

Very likelyRahadin catches us, or blocks the way out

Why it may come up: Strahd is away, but his chamberlain isn't. Sasha warned that "his right hand stalks the shadows," and that anyone he catches won't be allowed to leave (Session 48).

What we know

  • Strahd's chamberlain and "brother in all but blood… the most loyal creature I've ever had." He has served Strahd for centuries (Session 47).
  • A dusk elf with twin scimitars. He killed Sir Godfrey Gwilym in the Order's last battle (Session 41).
  • Mel's hidden blade points at him like a compass needle and hums harder when he's near (Sessions 47–48).
  • He locked us into the guest suite and posted Esher at the door (Session 48). In the dream, his cellar office held the golden key to Varushka's prison (Session 50).
  • Party theory: he's tied to the dream's dragon. It came with the same chorus of screams we once heard near him, and Mel's blade reacted to both (Session 51).

Think about

  • If he finds us, do we talk, run, or fight? Who speaks for us?
  • Is there anything he wants more than stopping us?
Very likelyFreeing Emil, and what he'll be like

Why it may come up: He's the reason we swore Mother Night's oath. It must be done before the day after the new moon, which is tomorrow, the 16th.

What we know

  • Zuleeka Tornesku's husband and the werewolf pack's rightful alpha. Kirill overthrew him 13 years ago using magic from Baba Lysaga (Session 40).
  • We swore to free him and restore him as alpha in exchange for the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind. If we miss the deadline, our max HP and stats start dropping daily (Session 41).
  • Strahd admitted holding him: "Kirill — a useful brute. The old alpha is resting. Hunger teaches obedience" (Session 47). He's likely starving.
  • Sasha: probably "in the dungeon, on the floor beneath the larders," by the South Tower stairs (Session 48).
  • Zuleeka and Ilya were held in silver-spiked collars whose key only Kirill holds (Session 41). Emil may be collared too.
  • Strahd once offered to trade Emil for Van Richten's location (Session 48).

Think about

  • How do we calm a starving werewolf? We're bringing meat.
  • If he's collared, how do we get it off without Kirill's key?
  • Freeing him isn't the whole oath. How do we get him back to the den to retake the pack from Kirill?
Very likelyRescuing Gertruda, and getting her to Doru

Why it may come up: Freeing her was Sasha's price, and Doru is outside waiting for her.

What we know

  • Mad Mary's daughter from the village of Barovia. She left for Vallaki with her horse Nori early in our time here, and vanished (Sessions 7–8).
  • Doru's betrothed. Doru and his father, Father Donavich, came to Vallaki looking for her (Session 46).
  • Caught on the road and held in the King's Suite, third floor, by the South Tower stairs. Sasha fears she won't live long there (Session 48).
  • Doru is a vampire spawn fighting the hunger. He agreed to make a diversion at the drawbridge, but fears he might relapse (Session 51).

Think about

  • Will she trust strangers breaking into her room? A message from Doru or her mother could help.
  • Is it safe to hand her to Doru if he's struggling with his hunger?
Very likelyTaking Argynvost's skull, and what comes after

Why it may come up: It's a main objective, and it unlocks the Sunsword.

What we know

  • Lord Argynvost was a silver dragon who founded the Order of the Silver Dragon. Strahd burned his bones and took his skull to Ravenloft as a trophy (Session 41).
  • The gargoyle's prophecy: "When the dragon dreams its dream, within its rightful tomb, the light of Argynvost will beam and rid this land of gloom" (Session 45). The skull goes back to his mausoleum at Argynvostholt.
  • Sir Godfrey will reveal where the Sunsword is once "Vladimir's final mission" is done (Session 41), so lighting the beacon matters.
  • Sasha: the Hall of Bones is behind the closed doors west of the kitchen (Session 48). In the dream, a dragon skull of about 250 lb hung above the guards' hall doors (Session 50).
  • Zorya, Argynvost's pseudodragon, waits at Argynvostholt (Session 43). Commander Vladimir Horngaard won't want the beacon lit (Session 45).

Think about

  • Will Flint's shrink gadget hold until we're out? Who carries the chest?
  • At Argynvostholt, how do we get past Vladimir to the mausoleum?

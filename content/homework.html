🗡️ Rahadin, Strahd's chamberlain

Why it matters: he runs the castle while Strahd's away, and we just read his farewell letter.

  • A dusk elf. Strahd calls him "my brother in all but blood" (S47).
  • Nearly five centuries ago he left "a prince that knew only weakness" to serve King Barov, Strahd's father, who adopted him (letter, S52).
  • He's mortal and expects to die: grateful "to serve you one final time… my eyes shall not see the glory of your rise from the ashes" (S52). What that last service is, we don't know.
  • He killed Sir Godfrey in the Order's last battle (S41).
  • He keeps the prisoners' keys: in the dream, the key to Varushka's room sat in his office (S50). There was no key there this time (S52).
  • When Strahd's away he "stalks the shadows," and anyone he catches won't be allowed to leave (Sasha, S48). He wasn't in his office at 11 p.m. (S52).
  • Mel's hidden blade points at him and hums (S47–48). The chorus of screams we hear near him also came with the dragon in the dream (S49).

🕯️ Why is Gertruda here?

Why it matters: freeing her is Sasha's price, and it's why Doru helped us tonight.

  • She's Mad Mary's daughter from the village of Barovia. Mary was frantically searching for her. She had set off for Vallaki with her horse, Nori (S8).
  • She's Doru's partner. Doru and Father Donavich came to Vallaki looking for her (S46).
  • She was caught on the road to Vallaki and is held in the King's Suite, 3rd floor (Sasha, S48). Why Strahd took her, we don't know.
  • Sasha fears she won't live long if she stays. Freeing her is Sasha's price for helping us, and Flint promised to try (S48).
  • Doru staged the diversion ("I will help save my Gertruda"), though he fears he might relapse (S51).

✨ The Icon of Dawn's Grace

Why it matters: it may be the artifact that restores the Abbott, and it could be somewhere in this castle.

  • What it is: a small silver statuette. In Argynvostholt's stained glass, an angel who looks like the Abbott gives it to Saint Markovia (S43).
  • Brother Marek named it and called it a vessel for a shard of divinity: "seek out the younger of the twin dragons above, reignite the silver flame it once wielded, reminded of the oath it once served" (S44).
  • The seance said restoring the Abbott needs an artifact found in the Amber Temple or "the dungeons of Ravenloft" (S39). We think the Icon is that artifact.
  • Below the chapel? Mel's Holy Symbol grew hotter the lower he went in the chapel, and Spiggot's feather slid under the door to the catacomb stairs (S48).
  • How to search now: the Symbol has gone cold (S52), so use Vel's Detect Magic and the avian-eye stone (three Clairvoyance uses, S51).

Why it matters: we walked these halls in the castle's past, and some of it still matches.

  • From the 4th-floor guest suite, the closet trapdoor took us further back in time with each trip. The clock bit us every hour (S49).
  • The night Strahd turned, the cellars ran with blood. Guards and a cook were torn open, and in the guards' hall Strahd fed on a guard and roared for "Dilisnya" (S50). Leo Dilisnya fled down the stairs and out of the castle (S50).
  • The ruined room before Rahadin's office looked like that night, only older and worse (S52). We remembered the kitchen and the way to his office from the dream (S52).
  • Varushka, a maid, was locked in her room for trying to escape. Her key was in Rahadin's office (S50).
  • Ismark the Great, in 662: Strahd "slumbers in the depths of this keep." In the crypts, the floor swept his companion Lugdanna up into the heights of the castle (S49).
  • A dragon forced its way out of the guest suite's window onto the roof, with the chorus of screams we know from Rahadin (S49).
  • Nothing we found in the dream came back with us (S50).

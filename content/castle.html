🎯 Objectives — where we believe they are

  1. 1
    ✓ Way in: the servants' entrance (Session 52). There were no wights on the east wall, so the ravens flew us up to the east overlook. We walked north past the carriage house and shouldered open the stuck door across from it. Inside are a dusty office with two skeletal guards at the stairs, then the servants' hall.
  2. 2
    Emil: "probably in the dungeon, on the floor beneath the larders," by the South Tower stairs (Sasha). The dungeon isn't on this map. He's being starved, so bring meat. Not confirmed; the avian-eye stone could check.
  3. 3
    Gertruda: the King's Suite, third floor, reached by the South Tower stairs from the foyer (Sasha).
  4. 4
    ✓ Argynvost's skull (Session 52): it hung above the east doors of the Hall of Bones, the steel-banded doors at the far end of the servants' hall from the wine cellar. Shrunk and packed in the puzzle chest. Taking it raised skeletons from the four bone mounds.
  5. 5
    Icon of Dawn's Grace: location unknown. Best guess: below the chapel, down the catacomb stairs behind the chapel's south door (D), in the part of the catacombs under the chapel. Mel's Holy Symbol grew hotter the lower he went in the chapel, Spiggot's feather slid under that door, and the seance said "the dungeons of Ravenloft" or the Amber Temple. The Holy Symbol has gone cold (S52), so use Detect Magic and the avian-eye stone instead.
  6. 6
    Sasha's chamber, behind the dining-hall organ (sixth pedal from the left). She mouthed "tonight," but has said she must report intruders she sees.

⚠️ Trouble — where it waits

  1. A
    Wights patrol the walls above the drawbridge. Doru's diversion goes here. The drawbridge is raised, and in Session 48 its runes needed the witches' coven to recast them.
  2. B
    Rahadin's office (found, Session 52): off a ruined 30-foot hall (the "cracked hall?"), and reached from the servants' hall. It was empty and in perfect order, with his farewell letter on the desk. A hidden door opens on a web-choked stair down, where a figure ducked out of sight. Rahadin himself "stalks the shadows" and won't let intruders leave (Sasha), and we don't know where he is.
  3. C
    The catacombs and Strahd's tomb. "Many have visited the catacombs, and they have not come out" (Strahd). "Don't get lost down there… or open the wrong door" (Van Richten).
  4. D
    The catacomb stairs behind the chapel's south door, which Strahd barred on the tour.
  5. E
    The brides' stairs. Before dinner, the brides and Esher went down the southern stairs off the great hall (Session 47). Their rooms are likely somewhere below.
  6. F
    The garrison (Session 52, not on this map): through a side door of the Hall of Bones, down a lichen-covered passage. 36 undead in nine alcoves, led by pale, red-veined ones that Turn Undead can't destroy, plus a zombie that bursts poison gas. We barricaded the door. Cyrus says the garrison connects to the servants' wing through the Hall of Bones.
  7. !
    Also inside: Ludmilla, Anastrasya ("fear the one who watches"), Volenta, Esher, Ludmilla's witches, and an undead garrison. The portcullises are loud (Van Richten's note).

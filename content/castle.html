🎯 Objectives — where we believe they are

  1. 1
    Way in: the servants' entrance (off the north courtyard). Sasha: the rear servants' door leads to the servants' quarters and kitchens. Muriel's ravens fly us to the north side.
  2. 2
    Emil: "probably in the dungeon, on the floor beneath the larders," by the South Tower stairs (Sasha). The dungeon isn't on this map. He's being starved, so bring meat. Not confirmed; the avian-eye stone could check.
  3. 3
    Gertruda: the King's Suite, third floor, reached by the South Tower stairs from the foyer (Sasha).
  4. 4
    Argynvost's skull: the Hall of Bones, behind the closed doors west of the kitchen (Sasha). In the dream it was mounted above the guards' hall doors near the kitchen and servants' hall: a large dragon skull, about 250 lb. Shrink it with Flint's gadget and pack it in the puzzle chest.
  5. 5
    Icon of Dawn's Grace: location unknown. Best guess: below the chapel. Mel's Holy Symbol grew hotter the lower he went, Spiggot's feather flew down the catacomb stairs, and the seance said "the dungeons of Ravenloft" or the Amber Temple. Use Detect Magic.
  6. 6
    Sasha's chamber, behind the dining-hall organ (sixth pedal from the left). She mouthed "tonight," but has said she must report intruders she sees.

⚠️ Trouble — where it waits

  1. A
    Wights patrol the walls above the drawbridge. Doru's diversion goes here. The drawbridge is raised, and in Session 48 its runes needed the witches' coven to recast them.
  2. B
    Rahadin. Strahd's right hand "stalks the shadows" and won't let intruders leave (Sasha). In the dream his office was near the kitchens and the kingsmen hall.
  3. C
    The catacombs and Strahd's tomb. "Many have visited the catacombs, and they have not come out" (Strahd). "Don't get lost down there… or open the wrong door" (Van Richten).
  4. D
    The catacomb stairs behind the chapel's south door, which Strahd barred on the tour.
  5. !
    Also inside: Ludmilla, Anastrasya ("fear the one who watches"), Volenta, Esher, Ludmilla's witches, and an undead garrison. The portcullises are loud (Van Richten's note).
